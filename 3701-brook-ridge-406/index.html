Welcome Home

Move-in ready, with wooded views

3701 Brook Ridge Ct #406, Des Moines, IA 50317 is a cute, well-kept and move-in-ready townhome in a fantastic Des Moines location! Tucked away at the end of the street with wooded views instead of looking into other townhomes, this well-maintained 2 bedroom, 1.5 bathroom home offers maintenance-free living at its finest.

As you enter the front door you will appreciate the spacious, yet inviting living room that flows into the kitchen featuring bar-height seating, an optional dining space, and a half bath perfect for guests and main floor living. Upstairs you will love the large main bedroom featuring two generous closets, the second bedroom, a full bathroom and the conveniently located 2nd floor laundry.

The 1-car attached garage adds everyday convenience, while the location makes getting around easy with quick access to the interstate, retail, restaurants, and Adventureland. Whether you are a first-time buyer or looking to downsize, this low-maintenance, move-in-ready townhome is the one you don't want to miss!
