Welcome Home

Location, space & your own backyard oasis

806 Edgewater Drive, Polk City, IA 50226 has it all! Conveniently located near Big Creek State Park, Saylorville Lake, and TCI Golf Course, you will love having outdoor recreation just minutes away, along with Polk City's charming downtown and an easy drive to Ames, Ankeny and Des Moines.

This beautiful 4 bedroom, 3.5 bathroom home welcomes you with a grand entryway featuring vaulted ceilings and a spacious sitting room. The inviting living room features a gas fireplace surrounded by built-in shelving and flows seamlessly into the dining area and kitchen, complete with a pantry. Windows cover the back side of the home allowing natural light to fill the space while also providing the perfect backyard views overlooking the large deck, well-maintained pool (new filter/pump in 2025 & new liner 10/2026), fully fenced backyard and patio space perfect for entertaining and relaxing with friends and family.

Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features vaulted ceilings, a dual vanity and large walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and conveniently located second-floor laundry complete the upper level. The finished lower level offers even more living space with a family room, 4th bedroom and 3/4 bathroom. A large 3-car attached garage provides plenty of space for vehicles and storage.

This home allows the best of small-town living with incredible outdoor amenities and easy access to everything the Des Moines metro has to offer.
